02The problem

The job description says this role will “help shape complex post-trade journeys”, including securities transfers. That’s the one I picked.

A depot transfer goes on a trip the client can’t see.

The app→ Old broker→ Clearstream→ Custody and ops→ The app

for days, the client only sees “in progress”

Meanwhile the old account can look empty before the new one is full, and nobody says whether that’s normal.

So the same question travels through two layers of people.

Client→ Client Service→ Ops, who actually know

Missing purchase prices hurt later, at tax time.

If the old bank doesn’t send the cost basis and the client sells, the substitute rule (Ersatzbemessungsgrundlage) treats 30% of the sale amount as profit. An example:

With purchase prices
profit taxed: €1,000 €264 tax
Prices missing
profit taxed: €3,000 €791 tax

Bought for €9,000, sold for €10,000. Flat tax of 26.375% with solidarity surcharge and without church tax, rounded. The client can fix it in their tax return, but most only find out afterwards.

And every normal trade says “pending” for a while.

  • MonYou buy (T)
  • TueStill pending
  • WedSettled (T+2)

From October 2027 the EU moves to T+1, so this would settle on Tuesday.

04What I built

I built two connected screens, because a transfer and a trade both end in settlement and the client doesn’t care which one it is.

Where’s my trade? A trade tracker where the trades are your shares.

  1. Request sent
  2. Old broker got it
  3. Old broker sent the shares
  4. In transit through the CSD
  5. Arrived in your account
  6. Purchase prices received

Each step says in one sentence what’s happening and who’s on it. Every position has its own status, because an ETF can land on Tuesday and a US stock a week later.

Why does it say pending? It explains the gap between pressing Buy and settlement, and opens from anywhere the app says “pending”.

Ops view. The same transfer as a table: position, step, days in step and a flag when it’s late. The client screen and the ops screen read the same status.

Next, if there’s time: the Altersvorsorgedepot, from 1 January 2027. People will ask the same questions there. I sketched one moment of it separately: Handover at 18.

06Questions I’d bring to the team

  1. Which of the six steps do you get a signal for today, and which are silent?
  2. How long is “usual” for each step, and does it depend a lot on the old broker? My guess is yes.
  3. How often do purchase prices arrive late or not at all, and what happens then?
  4. Does Client Service already have something like the ops view, and would they trust it?
  5. For the Altersvorsorgedepot, what’s the first post-trade question you expect in January?
